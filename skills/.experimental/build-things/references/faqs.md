Eligibility is confirmed after submission and verification.

We want these easter eggs to be special, for the people that found them on their own, like you! If you do share, note that the link itself may be accessible, but rewards are limited and subject to eligibility, verification, and availability. Sharing the link does not guarantee that someone will receive a reward.

# What if I don’t receive a confirmation email?
Please check your spam or junk folder. If you don’t receive an email after a reasonable period, it may mean that you were not eligible, or the available rewards have already been claimed. 

# Can I participate more than once?
No. Participation is limited to one (1) redemption per person and email address.

# What if supplies run out?
Once all rewards have been claimed, no additional rewards will be offered.
